Before we can process your invoice, we need a signed and notarized lien waiver on file. This is a standard requirement for all subcontractor invoices on our projects.

Please download the appropriate form and guide below:

  • NCG Interim Lien Waiver (Fillable PDF) — Use this for progress payments. Fill in all fields, sign in ink, and have it notarized.
  • NCG Final Lien Waiver (Fillable PDF) — Use this for final payments. Fill in all fields, sign in ink, and have it notarized.
  • How to Complete Your Lien Waiver (Guide & Example) — Step-by-step instructions with a completed example.

Scan both pages of the completed waiver as a single PDF and reply to this email with it attached. You do not need to re-attach your original invoice.

We won't be able to release payment until we receive the completed waiver. Incomplete forms — missing notary seal, mismatched amounts, or wrong dates — will need to be resubmitted.

Questions? Reply to this email or contact us at invoices@northwindcg.example.
